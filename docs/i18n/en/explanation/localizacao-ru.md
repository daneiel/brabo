# Localization — Russian (`ru`)

General rules in [Localization](localizacao.md).

- **Plural:** `one`, `few`, `many` and `other` — four forms, and the interface
  requires all four in every plural key (1 → `one`, 2–4 → `few`, 5–20 → `many`,
  fractions → `other`).
- **Terminology:** glossary terms that stay in English in pt-BR
  (*handoff*, *gate*, *runner*) also stay in English in Russian, in the Latin
  alphabet.
- **Not translated:** Brabo, agent and role names, commands, paths,
  RN/ADR numbers. Do not transliterate proper names into Cyrillic.
