# Localization — English (`en`)

General rules in [Localization](localizacao.md).

- **Plural:** `one` and `other`. pt-BR's `_zero` has no counterpart in English.
- **Fixed terminology:** *handoff*, *gate*, *harness*, *runner*, *binding*,
  *lead* and *dev agent* stay as they are (they are already in the glossary). "Sessão" is
  *session*, "aprovação" is *approval*, "história" is *story*, "regra de
  negócio" is *business rule*.
- **Not translated:** agent names when they are proper names (Criativo,
  Arquiteto — the glossary gives the equivalent in parentheses on first
  occurrence), Brabo, commands, RN/ADR numbers.
