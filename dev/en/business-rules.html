    Business rules

    Each rule has a statement, where it lives (file:line), and the test that covers it. If you change a rule, update the line here in the same change — that's what .docmap.yml requires with block severity.

    They all live in apps/api/src/domain/, which is pure: no IO, no framework, no database. That's why each one has a fast, deterministic unit test.
